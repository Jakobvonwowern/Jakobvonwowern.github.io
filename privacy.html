Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how GreenCare Gardening collects and uses personal information submitted through the booking form.

What data we collect

We may collect your name, email address, selected service, and any message you choose to send through the form.

Why we collect this data

We collect this information only to respond to your request and arrange gardening or winter maintenance services.

How the data is stored

Booking information may be stored in a MariaDB database as part of the backend system.

Your rights

You may request access to your information or ask for your information to be deleted.
