Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how GreenCare Gardening collects and uses personal information submitted through the booking/contact form.

What data we collect

We may collect your name, email address, selected service, and any message you choose to send through the form.

Why we collect this data

We collect this information only to respond to your request and arrange gardening or winter maintenance services.

How the data is stored

In the current frontend version, the form validates your input but does not store the data permanently. In a later backend version, booking requests may be stored in an SQL database.

Data minimization

We only ask for information that is necessary to process a booking request.

Your rights

You may request access to your information or ask for it to be deleted.
